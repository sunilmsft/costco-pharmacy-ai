Guided Pharmacy Care

An outside-in exploration of how Costco Pharmacy could help members move from intent to action with clearer guidance, less searching, and less repetition.

This concept is intended to make a possible member experience tangible and support discussion. It is not a finished recommendation, production design, or commitment to a particular technology or architecture.

Start with the outcome, not the technology

A responsible next step could be to:

  1. Align on the member or business outcome.
  2. Select one bounded journey.
  3. Understand the current baseline and dependencies.
  4. Identify the required operational, clinical, technology, and governance partners.
  5. Decide whether a small validation is worth exploring.

The goal is not to begin with the most advanced experience. It is to learn where guided support can create meaningful value and earn the right to expand.

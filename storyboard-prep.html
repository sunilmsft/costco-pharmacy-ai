MEMBER PERSPECTIVE

Members arrive with different needs, urgency, and confidence

"I need this medication today."
Urgency — needs the fastest available path
👨‍👩‍👧
"I'm managing prescriptions for my family."
Responsibility — coordinating multiple people and medications
❓
"I'm not sure whether I need an appointment."
Uncertainty — needs help understanding the right next step
🏥
"I'm transferring from another pharmacy."
New to the process — needs clear guidance and expectations
The experience should respond to the member’s situation while remaining clear, calm, and supportive.
THE SHIFT

From navigation alone to guidance when it matters

In a navigation-first experience

The member interprets the available paths
  • Find the page or service that appears relevant
  • Read information across pages and flows
  • Determine which option fits their situation
  • Move between experiences as the journey changes
  • Seek help when the next step remains unclear

With guided support

The member receives help moving from intent to action
  • Ask in their own words what they need
  • Receive clear, plain-language guidance
  • See relevant options and next steps
  • Continue through a more connected journey
  • Reach pharmacy staff when judgment or reassurance is needed
WHAT IT ENABLES

Guidance can begin with the member’s situation

💊 Refill
"How do I refill my prescription?"
🔄 Transfer
"How do I move a prescription from another pharmacy?"
💉 Immunization
"Help me understand how to schedule the right vaccine."
🚗 Pickup or Delivery
"What options are available for receiving my medication?"
👨‍👩‍👧 Managing Prescriptions
"Help me understand how to manage prescriptions for my family."
Guided support helps members move from a question to the appropriate next step, without first needing to understand the site structure.
GUARDRAILS

Designed for trust from day one.

Clear boundaries
✓ Supports members without replacing pharmacist judgment
✓ No diagnosis or independent clinical advice
✓ Complements existing pharmacy workflows rather than replacing them
Built-in trust mechanisms
✓ Grounded in approved content and defined business rules
✓ Authentication before accessing or discussing personal information
✓ Privacy, consent, and auditability built in
✓ Clear handoff to pharmacy staff when needed
Proposed Phased Approach

Start focused, validate member value, then earn the right to expand

1
Recommended starting point
Guide
Validate the member need with public information, clear guidance, and navigation into existing flows.
"How do I transfer a prescription?"
Does guidance improve clarity and next-step success?
2
Assist
If Phase 1 demonstrates value, explore authenticated support and smoother transitions into pharmacy workflows.
"Help me refill this prescription."
Can it improve completion without adding risk or operational burden?
3
Explore proactive support
If aligned with member needs, consent, and Costco priorities, explore timely suggestions and broader support.
"You have refill options available to review."
Is proactive support trusted, useful, and wanted?

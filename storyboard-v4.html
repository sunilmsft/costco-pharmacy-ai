APPENDIX · NAVIGATION VS GUIDANCE

Navigation remains essential; guidance reduces decision effort

Navigation-first

Member maps their need to site structure
  • Find a likely service page
  • Interpret options and policy details
  • Decide whether to self-serve or call

Guided support

Member starts with intent, then moves to action
  • Ask in their own words
  • Receive clear next-step guidance
  • Enter the right workflow with context carried forward

Guided Pharmacy Support as a Member Effort Opportunity

This is an outside-in concept, not a finished recommendation. The goal is to explore whether guided support could make the member's next step easier, more relevant, and more connected.

NORTH STAR FIRST

The right guided experience depends on the outcome Costco Pharmacy wants to improve

Illustrative strategic directions (not assumptions about Costco Pharmacy priorities):

📈Growth
Acquire new prescriptions, simplify transfers, improve relevant service adoption, and convert member intent into completed actions.
😊Member Experience
Improve clarity and completion, reduce repeated effort, increase confidence and satisfaction, and make support easier to access.
⚙️Operational Efficiency
Increase successful digital self-service, reduce avoidable calls, improve resolution time, and carry useful context into handoffs.
Align on the North Star first, then select the member journey, experience, and measures most likely to support it.
WHAT THE EXPERIENCE COULD ENABLE

Begin with the member's situation and guide them to the appropriate next step

1. Understand the need

Start in the member's own words
  • Do not require the member to know site structure first
  • Do not require pharmacy terminology first

2. Use appropriate context

Reduce effort while preserving control
  • Use permitted context: saved preferences, prior choices, location, journey context
  • Offer defaults the member can change

3. Connect the next step

Guide into existing Costco workflows
  • Connect related journeys when relevant
  • Make pharmacy staff easy to reach when judgment is needed

Illustrative examples

Short examples only
  • Suggest the member's usual pharmacy, while allowing a different location
  • Suggest pickup or delivery based on saved preferences, while allowing changes
Use context to reduce effort, not to remove member control.
DESIGNED FOR TRUST

Trust and safety principles for a guided pharmacy experience

1. Clear boundaries
✓Supports members without replacing pharmacist judgment
✓No diagnosis or independent clinical advice
2. Responsible use of context
✓Approved information and defined business rules
✓Authentication, privacy, consent, and auditability
✓Context reduces effort without removing member control
3. Intentional human support
✓Handoff is a designed part of the experience, not a failure
✓Carry useful context so members do not repeat themselves
LEARN AND DECIDE

Start focused, measure, and earn the right to expand

1
Align
Confirm the North Star and target member journey.
2
Baseline
Understand current performance before introducing an MVP.
3
Test
Launch a narrow, responsible hypothesis.
4
Measure
Track outcomes continuously, including experimentation and A/B testing when appropriate.
5
Decide
Expand, iterate, change direction, or stop.
Illustrative measures
Successful next-step completion, abandonment, conversion/prescription acquisition, member satisfaction, avoidable call reduction, resolution time, handoff rate and quality.
Expansion principle
Begin with guidance + existing workflows, add deeper assistance only if it demonstrates value, explore proactive support only if trusted, useful, wanted, and aligned with Costco priorities.
Each phase earns the next through measurable member and business value, not simply because more technology is possible.

OPTIONAL FUTURE VISION

What could connected Costco member journeys eventually make possible?

Illustrative connected journey: Travel planning -> pharmacy next steps

Future implication, not part of the initial MVP
  • Travel destination and timing are understood
  • Relevant travel-health guidance is surfaced
  • Upcoming prescription needs are checked
  • Early refill or immunization path is offered when relevant
  • Member is connected directly to the right workflow or pharmacy staff support
  • The member continues without coordinating every step independently
The larger opportunity is continuity across Costco journeys from the member's point of view. Any broader use depends on evidence, consent, relevance, technical feasibility, and business-area priorities.

APPENDIX · NAVIGATION VS GUIDANCE

Navigation remains essential; guidance reduces decision effort

Navigation-first

Member maps their need to site structure
  • Find a likely service page
  • Interpret options and policy details
  • Decide whether to self-serve or call

Guided support

Member starts with intent, then moves to action
  • Ask in their own words
  • Receive clear next-step guidance
  • Enter the right workflow with context carried forward
